Short Bio

Welcome! I am a PhD candidate in Business Administration (Accounting) at the Smeal College of Business, Penn State University. My research focuses on financial reporting, auditing, disclosure, uncertainty, and capital market information environments.

During 2024-2025, I was a Visiting Research Intern in the Office of Economic and Risk Analysis at the Public Company Accounting Oversight Board. That experience supported the development of two active working papers on PCAOB inspection reports and audit quality.

Research

Request papers

  1. Knightian Uncertainty and Fair Value Estimation

    with Rick Mergenthaler

    Based on second-year paper at Penn State

    Abstract

    This project studies how uncertainty affects fair value estimation and the information conveyed through financial reporting.

  2. Climate Risk and Corporate Green Innovation: Evidence from Nearby Climate Disasters

    with Phong Truong

    Under review at Journal of Accounting Research

    Abstract

    This paper examines whether nearby climate disasters affect corporate green innovation, connecting climate risk to firm investment and disclosure-related decisions.

  3. PCAOB Inspection Reports and Information Dissemination

    with Andrew Acito, Amir Amel-Zadeh, James Anderson, William Anderson, Daniel Aobdia, Francois Brochet, Huaizhi Chen, Jonathan Fluharty-Jaidee, Martin Schmalz, and Manyun Tang

    Preparing for initial submission at Review of Financial Studies

    Abstract

    Based on my second-semester proposal at Penn State and developed during my time at the PCAOB. The project studies how PCAOB inspection reports disseminate information to market participants.

  4. Market-based Incentives for Optimal Audit Quality

    with Andrew Acito, Amir Amel-Zadeh, James Anderson, William Anderson, Daniel Aobdia, Francois Brochet, Huaizhi Chen, Jonathan Fluharty-Jaidee, Martin Schmalz, Manyun Tang, Joshua White, Thomas Bourveau, and William Zame

    Revise and resubmit at Management Science

    Notes

    Developed during my time at the PCAOB. The paper is cited in PCAOB Release No. 2024-012 on firm and engagement metrics and in the PCAOB Chair's address at the 2024 AICPA & CIMA Conference.
