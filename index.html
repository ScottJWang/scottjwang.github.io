Bio

Welcome! I am a PhD candidate in Business Administration (Accounting) at the Smeal College of Business, Penn State University. I am on the 2026-2027 accounting rookie job market.

My research examines how public institutions, disclosure choices, auditing oversight, and uncertainty shape capital market information environments. During 2024-2025, I was a Visiting Research Intern in the Office of Economic and Risk Analysis at the Public Company Accounting Oversight Board.

Research

Job Market Paper

[1] Behind Closed Doors: Presidential Meetings and Strategic Public Disclosure

Sole-authored job market paper

  • I study what drives strategic presidential disclosure and its capital-market implications using Presidential Daily Diaries and LLM-assisted topic identification.
  • Greater correspondence between presidential meetings and public communications is associated with lower subsequent aggregate and firm-level market uncertainty.
